# Center for Land Economics — Brand & Style Guide

The canonical, living guide to the whole CLE brand: how we sound *and* how we
look. It absorbs and supersedes the standalone visual deck
(`Style Guide/CLE_StyleGuide.pdf`, Lockstep Studio, June 2026), which remains
on file as the original designed reference. When this document and the PDF
disagree, this document wins.

- **Owner:** Greg Miller
- **Last updated:** 2026-07-21
- **Applies to:** everything externally facing — website, reports, decks,
  social, email, grant narratives, press.

> How to use this: designers and vendors work from *Visual identity* onward
> plus the asset map. Anyone writing in CLE's voice works from *Brand
> foundation* and *Verbal identity*. Read both before anything goes public.

---

## 1. Brand foundation

**Who we are.** The Center for Land Economics (CLE) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit
conducting original research and education on land economics and land value tax
(LVT) reform, and building open-source civic tooling. Two-person org (Greg
Miller, Executive Director; Lars Doucet, President of R&D) plus fellows.

**Mission (boilerplate — use verbatim).**

> The Center for Land Economics conducts research and provides education to
> promote equitable assessments and foster sustainable development for the
> benefit of communities.

**Positioning line.**

> Building fairer, more prosperous communities through centering land in
> economic policy.

**Core thesis.**

> The value of land returned to the community that makes it valuable.

**Personality.** Serious but approachable. We talk to legislators, journalists,
and funders as the informed peers they are — confident, specific, never
academic-for-its-own-sake and never strident. The visual brand carries the same
balance: a clean, modern, neutral system with one strong accent color, not a
loud one.

## 2. Verbal identity (voice)

CLE's voice is **direct, declarative, and specific.** We name the number, the
agency, the date, and the statute rather than gesturing at them. We reframe a
bad question instead of answering it. We are confident without dunking on
opponents.

Deeper references live in `brain/voice/`: `memo-style.md` (memos, briefs,
external writing) and `email.md` (Greg's email voice). Summary below.

### Voice principles

- **Specificity over generality.** "California's SB 220 (2026) Billionaire
  Wealth Tax," not "California's wealth-tax proposal." Name the foundation, the
  expert, the city, the dollar figure.
- **Lead with the answer.** Memos and briefs open with a two-to-four-paragraph
  executive summary a legislator can act on without reading further.
- **Reframe, don't hedge.** "We agree with the goal of progressive revenue; we
  disagree with the tool." Disagree with bad framing directly.
- **Cite everything external.** Any claim resting on outside evidence ends in a
  Sources section with full Markdown-linked URLs.
- **No filler.** Avoid *potentially, arguably, essentially, fundamentally, in
  many ways, various, some, a number of, it is worth noting that.* Make a
  concrete claim or drop it.
- **Not chatty, not pugilistic, not academic.** No "I think," no literature
  review for its own sake, no victory laps.

### Messaging hierarchy

1. **Tagline / rallying line:** *Land is a big deal.*
2. **Campaign line (property-tax reform):** *Shift Taxes to Land.*
3. **Positioning:** *Building fairer, more prosperous communities through
   centering land in economic policy.*
4. **Thesis:** *The value of land returned to the community that makes it
   valuable.*
5. **The one-liner explainer:** land is a limited resource and a vital input to
   economic activity; markets should reward productive *use* of land, not mere
   *ownership* — which is why the LVT is "the economist's favorite tax."

### Terminology & house shorthand

Use the CLE meaning for these, always — several collide with more common
expansions. Full disambiguations in `CLAUDE.md` and `brain/orgs/`.

| Term | Means | Not |
|------|-------|-----|
| **LVT** | Land value tax | — |
| **LVR** | Land value return (Lars/PPI framing in some contexts) | — |
| **LVC** | Land value capture | — |
| **PPI** | Progress and Poverty Institute (peer think tank) | Progressive Policy Institute |
| **PnP** | *Progress and Poverty* (our Substack, w/ PPI) | Plug and Play |
| **LLL** | LVT Landscape Live (webinar series) | — |
| **CLE** | Center for Land Economics | — |

### Capitalization & naming conventions

- **Organization name:** *Center for Land Economics* on first use, *CLE*
  thereafter. Never "the CLE."
- **Tagline capitalization:** sentence case — *Land is a big deal.* (The
  website hero renders it title-case as "Land is a Big Deal"; standardize to
  sentence case going forward — see *Proposed changes*.)
- **"land value tax"** is lowercase as a common noun; abbreviate to LVT after
  first use. Same for split-rate, building exemption.
- **Sentence case for headings** in memos, briefs, and this guide (`## What we
  found`), not Title Case.

### Channel voices at a glance

| Channel | Length & shape | Sign-off |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| **Memo / brief** | Exec summary first; sentence-case headings; Sources section | — |
| **Email (Greg)** | `Hey [First],` → warm + direct → forward-looking close | `Best,` / `Greg` (bare — no title block) |
| **Substack (PnP)** | Commentary voice; see `brain/voice/` | — |
| **Social / LinkedIn** | Short, claim-forward, one idea per post | — |

Email specifics (greeting, three length modes, punctuation tics like liberal
em-dashes and `!!` for warmth, anti-patterns) are in `brain/voice/email.md`.
Note for design: **the email signature is text only — `Best, Greg` — never a
graphical logo footer.**

## 3. Logo system

The 2026 refresh keeps CLE's square **icon mark** — a solid bar above an
outlined box, an abstract "ledger" / stacked-block form — but tightens spacing,
unifies line weights, drops the italic, and aligns the wordmark to the top of
the mark in a slightly bolder, more neutral face.

### Three lockups

| Lockup | Files | When to use |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Primary — full logo** | `CLE_Logo_*`, `CLE-Logo_Black` | Default. Icon + "Center for Land Economics" wordmark. |
| **Secondary — emblem** | `Icon+CLE_*` | Icon + compact "CLE" when the full wordmark is too much (avatars, tight headers). |
| **Icon only** | `Icon_*`, and containerized `CLE_Icon_1a`–`4a` | The bare mark, plus "in a circle" treatments (solid purple, light gray, with/without long shadow). |

Each lockup ships in **purple, black, white, and 2-color.** The **favicon** is
`CLE_Icon_1a` (white mark on a solid purple circle).

### Usage rules

- Give the logo clear space on all sides equal to the height of the solid bar
  in the icon; never crowd it.
- Don't recolor outside the four approved variants, stretch, rotate, add
  effects, or reconstruct the lockup by hand — pull the supplied file.
- On photos, use the white or 2-color version over the darker areas of the
  duotone treatment (see *Imagery*).
- Prefer **SVG** for web, **EPS/PDF** for print, **PNG** only when a raster is
  required.

## 4. Color

One strong accent (purple), black for text, and restrained backgrounds. The
core palette below is the brand law; the web-extended palette records the
additional tokens the live site uses so digital work stays consistent.

### Core palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Primary — **purple** | `#352977` | The brand color. Key accent moments, buttons, links. |
| Primary — **black** | `#000000` | Body and most text. |
| Background — **light gray** | `#EAE7E8` | Alternate to white; depth, dividers (text + photos). |
| Accent — **teal** | `#B7E3E0` | **Photos only**, paired with the dark purple. |

### Web-extended palette (live site, `cle-nextjs/app/globals.css`)

Derived from the palette sheet and in production on landeconomics.org. Treat as
the digital source of truth; see *Proposed changes* for formally blessing these.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| `--cle-purple-dark` | `#251C57` | Hover / pressed states of purple. |
| `--cle-accent` | `#3D358A` | Muted purple — eyebrows, quote rules. |
| `--cle-purple-tint` | `#EDECF5` | Light purple wash — selected rows, badges. |
| `--cle-blue` | `#5865F0` | Secondary interactive accent *(new; not in the Lockstep deck)*. |
| `--cle-teal` | `#7FC9C6` | Medium teal accent. |
| `--cle-teal-dark` | `#2F6F6C` | Teal text on light backgrounds. |
| `--cle-hero-bg` | `#D4DDE5` | Blue-gray hero background. |

### Rules

- **Purple is an accent, not a flood.** Use it for key moments, calls to
  action, and the mark — not as a large fill except in deliberate hero/cover
  panels.
- **Teal is photo-only** in the core system, paired with purple.
- **Orange is reserved exclusively for the Givebutter Donate button.** Do not
  introduce orange anywhere else.
- Official brand purple is `#352977` — the value the logo art ships with (every
  purple logo/icon SVG fills with `#352977`). Reconciled to the logo on
  2026-07-21; docs and code updated to match.

## 5. Typography — DM Sans

One family across the entire brand. Free via Google Fonts and Adobe Fonts;
uploadable to Canva. Weights are also bundled in the report's packaged fonts.

| Role | Weight |
|------|--------|
| Logo & headers | DM Sans **Bold** (700) |
| Subheaders | DM Sans **SemiBold** (600) |
| Body | DM Sans **Medium** (500) |

**Web settings in production:** headings `font-weight: 700`, `line-height:
1.15`, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`; body `font-weight: 500`. Italic DM Sans is
used sparingly for emphasis (e.g., *sustainable development*), not for running
text. DM Sans carries a full weight range (Extra Light → Black Italic) for
flexibility, but the three roles above are the defaults — don't drift.

## 6. Iconography & secondary graphics

The icon mark doubles as the primary graphic device. Approved treatments (from
the deck): the mark in a **solid purple circle** (with and without a long
shadow), and in a **light-gray circle** (with and without shadow); the
emblem versions add "CLE" beneath the mark. Keep secondary graphics in the core
palette; don't invent new iconographic styles for one-off pieces.

## 7. Imagery & photography

- **Subject:** aerial/overhead city views and skylines (the parcels-and-blocks
  read reinforces the "land" story). The site hero is a Pittsburgh skyline;
  banners have used Baltimore.
- **Treatment:** duotone — a **purple/blue-gray wash** over the photo. Pair
  teal (`#B7E3E0`) with dark purple (`#352977`) for lighter, airier panels.
- **Text over image:** keep type in white or purple over the darker regions;
  maintain contrast for legibility.
- **The mark as a window:** an approved treatment knocks the icon out of a photo
  in white (the "alternate image treatment" in the deck) — use sparingly, for
  covers and heroes.

## 8. Applications

How the system shows up in the wild. **The report appears here once, as the
single canonical example** (the source files it was duplicating have been moved
to `archive/` — see the asset map).

### Website — landeconomics.org

- Hero: sentence-case headline over a duotone skyline; purple primary buttons;
  eyebrow labels in muted purple accent.
- Pull-quotes: left purple rule (`--cle-accent`), italic; inverted variant for
  dark backgrounds.
- Cards lift slightly on hover; keyboard focus shows a 2px purple ring.
- Section rhythm alternates white and light-gray (`#EAE7E8`) backgrounds.

### LinkedIn / social banners

Two approved patterns: (1) duotone skyline with **"Shift Taxes to Land"** in
white + the full logo on a white panel; (2) light-teal band with the headline
in black + logo. Keep one idea per banner.

### Reports

- **Canonical example:** *Taxing Land Not Homes* —
  `Final Report/CLE_TaxingLandNotHomes_vf.pdf` (23 pp., laid out in the refreshed
  brand). Use it as the reference for report layout: DM Sans throughout, purple
  accents, duotone figures, light-gray section fields.
- Editable InDesign source is archived (not deleted); restore from `archive/`
  if a report ever needs re-setting.

### Email

Text signature only: `Best,` / `Greg`. No graphical footer, title block, or
disclaimer.

### Slides / decks

DM Sans, purple accent on white or light-gray, icon mark top-left or as a cover
window. Reuse the report's figure style for data slides.

## 10. Proposed changes / open items

Recommendations surfaced while consolidating this guide (Greg to decide):

1. **Purple reconciled ✅ (2026-07-21).** The official brand purple is now
   `#352977`, matching the logo art as delivered. Updated everywhere it was
   defined as `#352977`: the brand docs, the website token (`--cle-purple`),
   and the LVT Shift deck tokens.
2. **Formally bless the web-extended palette.** The blue `#5865F0`, the teal
   variants, hover/tint purples, and hero blue-gray live only in code today.
   Either adopt them into the official palette here, or align the site to the
   core four.
3. **The new blue (`#5865F0`) is a real addition** not in the Lockstep deck —
   decide whether it's an official secondary accent or web-only.
4. **Standardize the tagline capitalization.** Guide says *Land is a big deal.*
   (sentence case); the site hero uses title case. Pick one — recommend
   sentence case per house style — and apply site-wide.
5. **Teal has two values in play:** `#B7E3E0` (photo background, core) and
   `#7FC9C6` (medium teal, web). Document both roles explicitly so they're not
   used interchangeably.
6. **Add clear-space and minimum-size specs with visuals** — the deck implies
   spacing rules but doesn't diagram them; worth a one-page addition.
7. **Report de-duplication (done):** the InDesign working files + zip were
   moved to `archive/`; the final PDF is now the single in-folder example.
