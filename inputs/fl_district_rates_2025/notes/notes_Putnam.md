- TACD text only extracts in pypdf plain mode (layout mode returns nothing). One row per 3-digit code: BOCC, fire MSTU, school (LRE/discr/capital/debt), WMD, city, TOTAL. All 14 roll codes present.
- Components sum to TOTAL for every row. BOCC 8.7418, fire 1.1000 and city rates (Palatka 6.2397, Crescent City 8.7164, Interlachen 6.9018, Pomona Park 6.50, Welaka 5.50) match Table 1 2025 adopted.
