# Saint Johns 2025 rates
- PA "2025 Final Millages by Jurisdiction" (13 pages): long format, one line per (district, authority, rate) followed by "Taxing District NNN TOTAL x". Parsed all district blocks; every roll code has a block.
- Components were re-summed per district and match the printed TOTAL (any mismatch would be flagged in the note).
- County GF 4.4999, road 0.8444, health 0.016, fire 1.47, MSTUs and St Augustine 7.50 / St Augustine Beach 2.50 match Table 1 2025 adopted. City districts drop the county fire district where printed.
- School = Public Schools Local 3.248 + State 3.024 = 6.272. Generic reader's 4.4999 was just the county general fund line.
