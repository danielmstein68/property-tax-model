# Hernando 2025 district millage

**Source:** Hernando County PA millage image, https://hernandocountypa-florida.us/images/MillageRates/Millage%20Rates.png (linked from /MillageRates; saved as data/raw/fl_rates_2025/appraisers/hernando_millage_rates.png). Its "2025 Final" column gives CWES 14.2756, CWBE 20.7756 and CWWE 14.2756.

**Checks**
- County, from DOR Table 1: BCC 5.8891 + Health 0.1054 + Transportation Trust 0.8091 + EMS MSTU 0.9100 + Stormwater MSTU 0.1139 = 7.8275.
- School is 6.2650 (inputs/fl_school_millage_2025.csv).
- 14.2756 - 7.8275 - 6.2650 = 0.1831, which is the 2025 SWFWMD rate (it is also in the Sarasota 2025 list). The total reconciles exactly.
- CWBE - CWES = 6.5000, which is Brooksville's adopted 2025-26 millage (DOR Table 1).

**Coverage:** the roll has only CWES and CWBE, so 100% of 61,732 homesteads are covered. CWWE (Weeki Wachee) is also written. Confidence high.
